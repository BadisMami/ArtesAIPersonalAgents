# Research Gap Identifier (`/gaps`)

## When to Apply This Skill
Trigger this skill when the user types `/gaps` or asks to identify research gaps, contradictions, limitations, or future project directions based on the provided literature.

## Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Analyze the user-provided papers across three gap categories:
   - **Population / Context Gaps**: Settings, regions, or populations not yet covered by these studies.
   - **Methodological / Scale Gaps**: Temporal, spatial, or analytical limitations across the studies (e.g., short timeframes, reliance on self-report, coarse resolution, lack of field validation).
   - **Unresolved Contradictions**: Places where two or more provided papers reach different conclusions or use incompatible metrics.
2. Propose **2 Concrete Follow-Up Research Questions** that a researcher could pursue next, explicitly stating which papers in the set motivate each question.
3. Draft a **3-Sentence "Gap Statement"** that bridges what is known `(citing the provided papers)` to what remains unknown, ready to adapt into a manuscript or grant introduction.
